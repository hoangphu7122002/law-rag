# Findings: small-generator robustness to noisy / expanded context (from subagent; search snippets only, numbers NOT verified; Self-RAG / lost-in-the-middle items are from memory)

- RAFT (https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10131): train with question + oracle doc + distractors (4 in the paper), CoT answer from the oracle; oracle kept for a fraction P of examples, the rest distractors only (forces memorisation). Multiple oracles possible. For us: mix gold-present and gold-dropped rows; a refusal target on gold-dropped rows is our own addition (RAFT answers there).
- RAAT (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20978, ACL 2024): separates relevant / irrelevant / counterfactual noise; adaptive adversarial training + multi-task noise-type head; improves F1/EM on LLaMA-2 7B under noise; no ROUGE.
- RobustRAG (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15556): answer from each passage in isolation then aggregate; certified robustness against injected passages; k LLM calls per query; training-free.
- Refusal / unanswerable: RSRR 2026 reward shaping on small models: +43.3% relative refusal accuracy, +39.8% distractor robustness; prompting and plain instruction tuning insufficient (https://staff.fnwi.uva.nl/m.derijke/wp-content/papercite-data/pdf/rajapakse-2026-reward.pdf). GRACE: answerable/unanswerable pairs by withholding evidence, gated RL (https://arxiv.org/html/2601.04525). Frozen 3.8B-8B models abstain OK with no context but collapse under misleading context (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.22228). Over-refusal: in-context fine-tuning reduces it, but better refusal != better calibration/accuracy (https://arxiv.org/html/2509.01476v3). Benchmarks: RGB, RefusalBench (https://arxiv.org/html/2510.10390v1), UAEval4RAG (https://arxiv.org/html/2412.12300v3).
- From memory, unverified: Self-RAG reflection tokens trained on critic labels (7B/13B); Lost in the Middle U-shaped position effect (randomise gold position in training); no ROUGE-based noise-robustness source found.

Implications for law-rag (<4B): SFT mix of (a) gold only, (b) gold + shuffled hard distractors (same-topic wrong articles, stale versions), (c) distractors only with refusal target, (d) gold dropped in round 1 and recovered in the expanded round 2. Evaluate by condition (no evidence / gold / gold+noise) with a position sweep; report ROUGE-L plus refusal precision/recall to catch over-refusal. Train refusal behaviour; prompt-only abstention is weak on small models.
