# Findings: datasets and AITeamVN recipe (agent summary; [unverified] = search summary only)
- AITeamVN/Vietnamese_Embedding: bge-m3 fine-tune, ~300k (query, pos, neg) triplets, max len 2048, dim 1024, Apache-2.0. Not disclosed: data source, hard-negative method, lr, batch, epochs; data not released. Zalo legal 2021: Acc@1 .7274, Acc@10 .9568, MRR@10 .8181 (raw bge-m3: .5682 / .6822; BKAI bi-encoder .7109 / .7951). The card says training data includes Legal Zalo 2021 → in-domain numbers. Embedding_v2: 1.1M triplets, slightly lower on legal, better on other domains.
- AITeamVN/Vietnamese_Reranker: bge-reranker-v2-m3 fine-tune, ~1.1M triplets, max len 2304 (query 256, passage 2048). Zalo legal: Acc@1 .7944, Acc@3 .9324, Acc@10 .9740, MRR@10 .8672. Recipe not reproducible from the card.
- Zalo AI Legal Text Retrieval 2021: ~61k corpus articles, ~2.5k train queries (NAACL 2025 paper: 2,556 train / 640 eval) [unverified]; HF mirror GreenNode/zalo-ai-legal-text-retrieval-vn [unverified]; licence unclear.
- BKAI synthetic (arXiv 2412.00657): Llama-3 queries, ~500k pairs, 165k train queries over a TVPL-derived benchmark [unverified]; HF link unconfirmed (bkai-foundation-models/crosslingual has VNLAWQC / VNSynLawQC, viewer errored).
- ALQAC 2021–2025: small, by registration; 2025 rules bar externally annotated legal-QA sets (check against the lecturer's OK). VLQA / VLSP 2025 DRiLL: >3,000 citizen questions over ~59k provisions, release status unverified (arXiv 2507.19995). ViHERMES: healthcare regulations, multi-hop, off-target (arXiv 2602.07361). HF: thanhdath/vietnamese_legal_retrieval gated; bqbbao6/vietnamese-legal-embedding (e5-base, 250k legal triplets, NDCG@10 .8761 on Zalo, self-reported). mMARCO-vi machine-translated, noisy, MS-MARCO (non-commercial) terms.
