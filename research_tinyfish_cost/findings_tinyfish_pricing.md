# Findings: TinyFish pricing (agent summary; official page https://tinyfish.ai/pricing; docs.tinyfish.ai/pricing 404)
- Pay-as-you-go, USD: Search (TinySearch) $0.00 per request, limits 30/min and 500/hour; Fetch (TinyFetch) $0.00 per URL, 150 URLs/min and 1,000/day; Agent (TinyAgent) $0.016 per step, 2 concurrent; Browser $0.002 per minute, 5 concurrent; Monitor $0.005/check, Research $2/run, Sets $0.10/row.
- Search and Fetch are free at any wallet balance within rate limits (blog: tinyfish.ai/blog/search-and-fetch-are-now-free-for-every-agent-everywhere).
- New accounts start with $8 wallet funds; minimum deposit $10; no "Hobby" tier named on the page (the 30/min, 150/min figures match pay-as-you-go).
- Not stated: top-up multipliers (the dashboard shows a 1.15x offer), cost variation with result count ($0 anyway). 1,000 searches = $0 but ~2 h at 500/hour.
- Third-party $150/month plan listing is probably outdated.
