# Research plan — are ROUGE-L ≈ 0.8 / 0.9 numbers reported for Vietnamese legal RAG / QA?

Main question: do published systems (papers, GitHub, Kaggle, course assignments) report ROUGE-L around 0.8–0.9 on Vietnamese legal QA / RAG, and under what conditions? Our system: ROUGE-L ≈ 0.66 dev (reference answers = "Căn cứ tại khoản N Điều M Thông tư X quy định …" + near-verbatim quote; <4B LLM).

Subtopics (3 parallel agents, 3–5 searches each):
1. **Published Vietnamese legal QA / RAG numbers** — ALQAC, Zalo AI legal, VLSP, ViLQA, ViLegalBench, URAx-Law, AITeamVN (Vietnamese_Embedding / Vi-Qwen2-3B-RAG / ViLegalQwen), Vietnamese legal RAG papers: which metrics (ROUGE-L F1/recall, BLEU, BERTScore, LLM-judge), what values.
2. **Code / assignment solutions** — GitHub, Kaggle, HF, blogs: Vietnamese law RAG projects; HCMUT RAG assignment baseline (Qwen1.5-1.8B + vietnamese-sbert + RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter 2000) and what people report after improving it.
3. **What ROUGE-L levels are reachable and why** — extractive/copy answers vs generated, references generated by an LLM from the retrieved article, fine-tuning on the same references; typical ROUGE-L for legal QA in other languages (so 0.8–0.9 is judged against a base rate); metric variants (F1 vs recall, token vs char, ROUGE-Lsum).

Synthesis: a table of (system, metric variant, value, source URL, verified?) + a verdict on whether 0.8–0.9 is a credible target for our setup, saved to docs/research/rougel-benchmarks/research_report.md. Findings are marked UNVERIFIED when read from snippets only.
